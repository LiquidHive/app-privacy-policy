# Privacy Policy for Audilator

**Last updated: May 28, 2026**

## Data Collection and Usage

Audilator respects your privacy. This app does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

### Speech Recognition

Audilator uses on-device speech recognition to process voice commands. Voice data is processed locally on your device and is **not** sent to any external server. No voice recordings are stored or shared.

### Advertising

Audilator uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data for ad personalization, including:

- Device advertising ID
- IP address
- App usage data
- Device type and OS version

Google's privacy policy applies to data collected by AdMob. You can view it at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

### In-App Purchases

Audilator offers a "Remove Ads" in-app purchase via Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play and your payment information is never accessible to this app.

### Local Storage

Audilator stores the following data locally on your device using SharedPreferences:

- App settings (theme, speech language, volume, rate, pitch)
- Whether ads have been removed via purchase

This data never leaves your device.

### Permissions

- **Microphone**: Used exclusively for on-device speech recognition. Only accessed when you tap the microphone button.
- **Internet**: Required for ad serving via Google AdMob.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google AdMob** – serves advertisements
- **Google Play Billing** – processes in-app purchases

Updates will be posted here. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
