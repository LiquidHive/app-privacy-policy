# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 27, 2026**

## Overview

Holy Scriptures ("the App") is a Bible study application. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how the App operates.

## Data Collection

**The App does not sell or share your personal data with third parties.** Specifically:

- **No analytics**: The App does not use any analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics).
- **No crash reporting**: The App does not use any crash reporting services.
- **Advertising**: The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects and processes data (such as your advertising identifier and device information) to serve ads. Google's Privacy Policy governs how this data is handled.

## Account and Cloud Sync (Optional)

Cloud sync is an **optional** feature. You can use the App fully offline without ever creating an account.

If you choose to enable cloud sync by signing in with Google:

- **Authentication**: Your Google account email and display name are used solely for authentication via Firebase Authentication.
- **Synced data**: The following data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore and associated with your account:
  - Bookmarks and bookmark folders
  - Notes
  - Daily verses
  - App preferences (theme, font settings, reading history)
  - AI chat sessions
- **Purpose**: This data is stored to sync across your devices when you sign in with the same Google account.
- **Control**: You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time from within the App (Settings → Cloud Sync → Delete Account).

## Data Storage

- **Bible text**: Bundled with the App and stored locally on your device.
- **User data without sync**: All preferences, bookmarks, notes, daily verses, and reading history are stored locally on your device using Hive and SQLite databases.
- **User data with sync**: If you enable cloud sync, your data is also stored in Firebase Firestore as described above.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

- **Google AdMob** (Google): Used to display advertisements. Google's Privacy Policy applies to data collected for ad serving.
- **Firebase Authentication** (Google): Used for optional sign-in. Google's privacy policy applies to authentication data.
- **Firebase Firestore** (Google): Used for optional cloud data sync. Google's privacy policy applies to stored data.
- **Google Generative AI (Gemini)**: Used for the optional AI Bible Study feature when you provide your own API key. No app data is sent to Google unless you explicitly send a chat message.
- **PayPal donation link**: Opens in your browser when you choose to support the developer.
- **Email feedback link**: Opens your default email client when you choose to send feedback.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the App and in this document.
