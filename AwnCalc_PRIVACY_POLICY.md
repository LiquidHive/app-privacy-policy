# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 30, 2026**

## Overview

AwnCalc ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### No Personal Data Collected by Us

AwnCalc does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data you enter (item prices, measurements, quantities, settings) is stored **locally on your device** using Hive database storage. We have no servers and do not access this data.

### Third-Party Advertising

AwnCalc uses **Google Mobile Ads** to display banner advertisements. Google may collect certain information as described in their privacy policy, including:

- Device identifiers
- General location data (non-precise)
- Ad interaction data

This data is handled entirely by Google and is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## How We Use Data

- **Local app data** (prices, measurements, settings) is used solely to provide the calculator functionality within the app.
- **Ad-related data** is used by Google to serve relevant advertisements.

## Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored locally on your device using encrypted Hive databases. No data is transmitted to external servers.

## Your Choices

- **Remove Ads**: You may remove banner advertisements through an in-app purchase.
- **Clear Data**: You can clear all app data through your device settings.
- **Opt Out of Ads**: You can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

## Children's Privacy

AwnCalc does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top.
